How to write a script 
# title: Caption at the top (meme text)
# bg: bedroom      (see Places)
# cast: hero, devil, angel   (who is on screen, left → right)

hero (happy): Hello!               face + line
hero (angry) [point]: You!         pose for this line
devil (smug) [zoom]: Hehe.         dramatic zoom on face
hero (shocked) [handsup shake]: !  combine tags
hero (sad) [3s]: ...               force line length
hero (sleepy) [cross]              change face/pose silently
- pause 1                          1 second of silence
// comment                         ignored

A pose lasts until that character's next line.

WALKING
hero [enter]                 walk in from the nearest side
hero [enter-left]: Hey guys! walk in from the left while talking
hero [exit-right]            walk off to the right
cop [pass]                   walk across the screen (left → right)
cop [pass-right]             walk across (right → left)
hero [goto bestie]           walk up next to bestie
hero [back]                  walk back to his own spot
hero [run exit]              add "run" to go faster
hero [walk]: ...             walk on the spot
Add a character to "# cast" so they have a spot to walk to.

CROWD & SOUND (best with # bg: comedyclub)
- laugh 2                    audience laughs for 2 seconds
- clap 2                     audience claps
- rimshot                    drum "ba-dum-tss"
hero [mic]: ...              hold a microphone (also mic-point, mic-shrug)

🤖 AI voices
Uses Kokoro, an open-source voice model (Apache 2.0) that runs on your own computer, in this browser. Each character has their own voice and pitch — change them in the Characters tab. The first time downloads about 90 MB; after that it loads from the browser cache.
Your own voice always wins: record (🎙) or upload (📁) a clip for any line. 🤖 = AI voice, 🔊 = your recording. The mouth follows whichever voice is used.
